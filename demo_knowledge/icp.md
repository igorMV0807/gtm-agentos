# Ideal Customer Profile

Northstar Revenue OS is designed for B2B software companies with 30 to 500 employees. The strongest fit is a recurring-revenue business with a small or mid-sized sales team, a documented pipeline, and a need to improve lead response consistency.

Priority buyers include Heads of Sales, Revenue Operations leaders, Sales Operations managers, and founders who still oversee revenue. Strong intent signals include hiring sales representatives, replacing spreadsheets, introducing a formal qualification process, or consolidating disconnected go-to-market tools.

Disqualifying signals include consumer-only businesses, teams without a repeatable sales motion, requests for mass unsolicited outreach, and companies seeking a full CRM replacement. Enterprise accounts above 500 employees require a manual architecture review before a commercial recommendation.
