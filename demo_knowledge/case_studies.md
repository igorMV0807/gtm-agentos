# Fictional Case Studies

Beacon Metrics, a fictional 90-person B2B analytics company, used a four-week pilot for inbound demo requests. The team standardized qualification fields and reduced the share of leads requiring manual triage from 62 percent to 28 percent. These figures are illustrative portfolio data, not a customer claim.

Harbor Cloud, a fictional 240-person infrastructure software company, used internal playbooks to give sales representatives a consistent research brief. Managers reported fewer unsupported claims in outreach drafts because the workflow exposed the exact internal sources used for each brief.

Use these examples only as demonstrations of possible workflow outcomes. Never represent either organization as a real customer, and never guarantee that another company will achieve the same results.
