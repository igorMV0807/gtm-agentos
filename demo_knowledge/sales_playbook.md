# Sales Playbook

For a qualified Head of Sales, open with the operational problem rather than a generic AI pitch. Confirm how leads arrive, how quickly they receive a response, who owns qualification, and where the team records the reason for each decision.

For companies with 30 to 150 employees, recommend a focused pilot around one lead source and one sales team. Define a baseline for response time, qualified-lead conversion, manual review rate, and duplicate records. Review pilot results after four weeks before expanding scope.

Discovery questions should be specific: How many inbound leads arrive each month? Which fields are reliable? What makes a lead sales-ready? Which decisions require a manager? What must remain visible for compliance or coaching? Do not imply guaranteed revenue outcomes.
