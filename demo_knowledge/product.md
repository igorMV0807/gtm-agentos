# Product and Positioning

Northstar Revenue OS is a fictional workflow layer for B2B revenue teams. It captures inbound lead data, applies a controlled qualification policy, routes leads to the correct next step, and preserves an audit trail for every automated decision.

The product complements an existing CRM rather than replacing it. Its core value is consistent execution: sales teams can see why a lead was classified, which internal playbook informed a recommendation, and what action should happen next.

The standard package supports one revenue team, configurable qualification rules, internal knowledge retrieval, and monthly workflow reviews. Pricing is discussed after confirming team size, workflow volume, security requirements, and integration scope. Never quote an unapproved price or promise an integration that is not documented.
